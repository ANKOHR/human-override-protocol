# Lessons for KOML

An intervention protocol needs a distinction between an operator decision and eligibility to act. Approval alone fails when scope, role, independence or validity dates do not match. The synthetic fixture validates that these independently planted cases can be found with exact reconciliation; it does not validate identity authenticity or infrastructure safety.

The assumption that deterministic eligibility could be audited from local files was validated on the planted fixture. The assumption that this saves operator time remains untested. A corrupted request changes the computed result, while corrupted expectation metadata does not, demonstrating that the report computes from requests.

Operating rule: separate input validity, policy decisions and execution authority, and never claim a productivity benefit without a recorded real baseline. Final product verdict stays pending until the two-week trial supplies evidence.
